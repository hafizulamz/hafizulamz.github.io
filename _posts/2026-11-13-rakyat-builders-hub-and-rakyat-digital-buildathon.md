MyDIGITAL Corporation, under the Ministry of Digital, has launched the [Rakyat Builders Hub](https://rakyatdigital.gov.my/rakyat-builders), part of the government's ambition for Malaysia to become an AI nation by 2030. The hub gives students a space to use AI to build the next Made by Malaysia solution.

It is a central platform where Malaysians can:

- set up a builder profile
- find builder events near them
- learn how to use AI to build solutions to real problems
- share those solutions with others on the hub

## From learning to building

1. **Learn:** build your foundation in AI, including AI tools, security, ethics and responsible use.
2. **Participate:** join AI building sessions, workshops and community events to exchange ideas with other builders.
3. **Create:** turn an idea into a working prototype, application, automation or AI-powered solution to a real problem.
4. **Showcase:** register and share your project, and show how it can make a difference.
5. **Grow:** explore ways to improve your solution, collaborate with others and take your idea further.

## Rakyat Digital Buildathon: 17–19 November

The first Rakyat Digital Buildathon takes place at Smart Nation Expo 2026.

**Dates:** 17–19 November 2026 (choose one session)  
**Venue:** MITEC, Kuala Lumpur  
**Who can join:** everyone, students and lecturers alike, whatever your technical background

Each session is a four-hour sprint where you learn how to use AI to vibe-code a solution to a problem you choose. Every participant also gets one month of free access to [ILMUCode](https://www.ilmu.ai/ilmucode/), Malaysia's homegrown AI coding platform.

Slots are limited, so register early.

### How to join

1. Visit [rakyatdigital.gov.my](https://rakyatdigital.gov.my/rakyat-builders), select **Rakyat Builders Hub** and click **Join the Rakyat Builders Hub**.
2. Create an account or log in with MyDigital ID, and set up your profile.
3. Go to the **Events** tab on your dashboard.
4. Select **17–19 Nov: Rakyat Digital Buildathon** and click **Register**. You can also go straight to the [event page](https://builder-hub.rakyatdigital.gov.my/events/hub/mydigital-x-krackeddevs-ai-build-a-thon-at-smart-nation-bm91).
5. Choose your preferred session.

Bring your laptop, charger and an idea you want to build.
