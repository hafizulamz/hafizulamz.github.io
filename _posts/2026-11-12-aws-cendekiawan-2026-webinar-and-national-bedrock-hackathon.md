AWS CendekiAwan is a free digital upskilling programme that equips university students with AI knowledge and skills. It is run by Elitery with AWS, supported by Elite Academy, UTAR and the UTAR Computer Society, with prizes sponsored by GDEX.

This year UTAR students can learn generative AI with PartyRock, build an app in a team of five, and compete for an **RM10,000 prize pool for UTAR teams**. The best teams go on to the National Bedrock Hackathon at the AWS office in Kuala Lumpur.

I am the UTAR coordinator for the programme, so feel free to reach out if you have questions.

## Learn PartyRock in about an hour

No experience is needed. The self-paced lesson runs on your phone through the AWS CendekiAwan Telegram bot, and you can finish your first generative AI app in about an hour. It is completely free, and you earn a certificate and a project for your portfolio.

1. Install Telegram from the App Store or Google Play, and sign in.
2. Open the bot, [t.me/AWS_Cendekiawan_Elitementor_Bot](https://t.me/AWS_Cendekiawan_Elitementor_Bot), and tap **Start**.
3. Register and follow the lessons.
4. Submit your PartyRock app through the bot.

The bot also links to the self-learning guidelines, which cover registration, the learning modules and the helpdesk.

## National Bedrock Hackathon 2026

Learn, build and scale your AI innovation nationwide. Teams build a PartyRock app on one of five themes:

- Smart Education
- Digital Health
- Environment & Climate
- Financial Inclusion & SME
- GovTech & Social Impact
